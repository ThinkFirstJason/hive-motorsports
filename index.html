01 — THE HIVE MENTALITY

ONE SWARM.
ONE DRIVE.

Hive Motorsports was built on the track and in the garage — by drivers, wrenches, and sim racers who believe speed is a team sport. A hive doesn't work alone. Every driver, every crew member, every fan in the stands is a cell in the same structure — small on their own, relentless together.

We race what we build and wear what we race in. No corporate polish, no pretending — just the grind of car culture, the discipline of motorsport, and a community that shows up every single weekend, virtual or asphalt.
